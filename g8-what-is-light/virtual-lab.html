🧪 Rainbow lab

LabSplit light + see infraredTeamworkSelf-Regulation and Discipline

You need: a clear glass of water · white paper · a torch (or sunlight) · a TV remote · a phone camera

Method

  1. Part A: stand the glass of water at the edge of a desk, on the white paper.
  2. Shine the torch (or sunlight) through the water at an angle. Move the paper until you see a band of colours.
  3. Record the colours from top to bottom.
  4. Part B: point a TV remote at a phone camera and press a button. Look with your eyes, then at the phone screen.

Safety: never look directly at the Sun or shine a torch into anyone's eyes. Keep water away from sockets.
