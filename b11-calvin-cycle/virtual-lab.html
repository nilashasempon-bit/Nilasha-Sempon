🖥️ Lights off!

SimulationWhat happens in the dark?Critical ThinkingNumeracy Skills

The Calvin cycle is called light-independent, but does it really work in the dark? The light reactions make ATP and NADPH, which power the Calvin cycle. Switch the light off, watch what happens to ATP, NADPH, RuBP and 3-PGA, then switch it back on.

🖥️ C3, C4 or CAM?

SimulationDesert adaptationsCritical ThinkingLoyalty and National Belonging

C4 plants separate the steps in space: mesophyll cells fix CO₂ into a 4-C molecule, which moves to bundle sheath cells and releases CO₂ for the Calvin cycle. Stomata partly close to conserve water. Maize, grasses, sugarcane.

🧪 Floating leaf disks

LabDoes CO₂ speed up photosynthesis?Numeracy SkillsTeamwork

You need: spinach leaf disks · 2 clear cups · water · baking soda (a source of CO₂) · a drop of soap · a syringe · a lamp

Method

  1. Cup A: water + a drop of soap. Cup B: water + baking soda + a drop of soap.
  2. Use the syringe to pull the air out of the leaf disks so they sink.
  3. Put 10 disks in each cup under the lamp.
  4. Count the floating disks every minute for 5 minutes. Oxygen made by photosynthesis makes them float.

Safety: keep water away from the lamp and its cable. Do not drink the solutions.
