🧪 Classroom lab: a nail electromagnet

LabHands-onTeamworkSelf-Regulation and Discipline

You need: a large iron nail · 1 m of insulated copper wire · a 1.5 V battery and holder · a box of paper clips

Method

  1. Wind 10 loops of wire around the nail. Connect the ends to the battery.
  2. Count how many paper clips the nail picks up. Disconnect the battery.
  3. Repeat with 20 and 30 loops. Record your results in a table.
  4. Conclusion: how does the number of loops change the strength?

Safety: disconnect the battery after each test. The wire can get warm.
