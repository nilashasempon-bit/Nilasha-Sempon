SimulationLook inside both cell typesCritical Thinking
Questions to discuss (with answers)
  • Which structures are in BOTH cells? → Plasma membrane, cytoplasm, ribosomes and DNA.
  • Which structures are ONLY in the eukaryote? → The nucleus and membrane-bound organelles such as mitochondria.
  • How is the DNA shaped? → Prokaryote: one circular chromosome. Eukaryote: several linear chromosomes.

🧪 Cheek cells vs yoghurt bacteria

LabObserve both typesCritical ThinkingTeamwork

You need: a compound light microscope · a clean cotton swab · plain yoghurt or laban · 2 slides and cover slips · methylene blue stain

Method

  1. Gently rub the swab inside your cheek and smear it on slide 1.
  2. Smear a tiny drop of yoghurt on slide 2.
  3. Add one drop of methylene blue to each, then a cover slip.
  4. Observe at 100×, then 400×. Record what you see.

Safety: use only your own swab and throw it away straight after. Stain can mark clothes and skin; wear gloves.
