Questions to discuss (with answers)
  • What changes from 1 to 2? → One oryx becomes many oryx: the same species, in the same area, at the same time.
  • What changes from 3 to 4? → The nonliving things are added: sun, water, sand and rocks.
  • Your turn: act it out! → One student stands (individual). Their row stands (population). The class stands (community). Point to the window and lights (ecosystem). Spread your arms (biosphere)!
