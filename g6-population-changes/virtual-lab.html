🖥️ Too many birds!

SimulationOverpopulation in a food chainTeamwork
Questions to discuss (with answers)
  • What happens to the spiders with 30 birds? → They crash: the birds eat them faster than they can reproduce.
  • Why do the lizards decrease too? → Lizards also eat spiders. With fewer spiders, there is less food for them.
  • What happens to the birds in the end? → They decrease too: overpopulation damages their own food supply.

🧪 Mice in winter

LabLess food, smaller populationTeamworkSelf-Regulation and Discipline

You need: 40 dried beans (the food) · 20 paper clips (the mice) · a cup · a pencil

Method

  1. Each mouse needs 2 beans to survive one season.
  2. Summer: put 40 beans in the cup. How many mice can survive?
  3. Winter: there is less food. Put only 16 beans in the cup.
  4. Flood: half of the winter food is washed away. Use 8 beans.

Safety: do not eat the beans or put them in your mouth. Wash your hands afterwards.
