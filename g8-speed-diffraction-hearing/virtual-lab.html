🧪 Breaking Waves lab

LabWater waves through gapsTeamworkSelf-Regulation and Discipline

You need: a shallow tray of water · a wooden dowel or ruler · two blocks · a small object (e.g. a stone)

Method

  1. Place the two blocks across the tray with a wide gap between them.
  2. Wave-maker: gently roll the dowel once to make a straight wave.
  3. Observer: watch the wave after it passes through the gap.
  4. Repeat with a narrow gap, then with a small object instead of the blocks. Record below.

Safety: keep water away from electrical sockets and wipe up spills straight away.
