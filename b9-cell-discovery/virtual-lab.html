Questions to discuss (with answers)
  • 10× eyepiece and 40× objective? → Total magnification = 10 × 40 = 400×.
  • Magnification vs resolution? → Magnification makes the image bigger; resolution makes it clear and detailed. A blurry image at 400× is not useful!
  • Three lenses: 5×, 5× and 7×? → Multiply them: 5 × 5 × 7 = 175×.

🧪 Onion cell lab

LabTotal magnificationNumeracy SkillsTeamwork

You need: a compound light microscope · a prepared onion cell slide · a pencil

Method

  1. Place the slide on the stage. Start with the 4× objective.
  2. Focus with the coarse knob, then the fine knob.
  3. Switch to 10×, then 40×, using only the fine focus.
  4. Calculate the total magnification (eyepiece × objective) and count the cells you can see.

Safety: carry the microscope with two hands. Never use the coarse focus at 40×; it can break the slide.
