🖥️ Phospholipids in water

SimulationWatch the bilayer formCritical Thinking
Questions to discuss (with answers)
  • Why do the heads face outward? → The heads are polar (hydrophilic): they are attracted to the water outside and inside the cell.
  • Why do the tails hide in the middle? → The tails are nonpolar (hydrophobic): they are repelled by water, so they face each other.
  • How many layers form? → Two: a phospholipid bilayer.

🧪 Oil, water & soap lab

LabWhy tails hide from waterCritical ThinkingTeamwork

You need: a clear cup of water · cooking oil · liquid soap · a spoon · food colouring (optional)

Method

  1. Add 2 spoons of oil to the water. Stir, then wait 30 seconds.
  2. Record what happens to the oil.
  3. Add a few drops of soap and stir gently.
  4. Record what happens now. Soap molecules, like phospholipids, have a water-loving head and a water-hating tail.

Safety: do not taste anything. Wipe up spills straight away; oil makes the floor slippery.
