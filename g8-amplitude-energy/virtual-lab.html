🧪 Amplitude lab

LabSmall vs big shakeTeamworkSelf-Regulation and Discipline

You need: a coil spring toy or a skipping rope · a small piece of ribbon or tape · the floor

Method

  1. Lay the spring/rope on the floor. Tie the ribbon to the middle.
  2. Holder: keep one end still. Shaker: move the other end side to side once.
  3. Observer: how far does the ribbon move from its rest position? That is the amplitude.
  4. Repeat with a small then a big movement. Record below.

Safety: never let go of a stretched spring. Keep it low on the floor.
