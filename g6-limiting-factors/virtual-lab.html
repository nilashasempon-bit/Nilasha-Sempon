🖥️ Population graph

SimulationBiotic potential vs carrying capacityTeamwork
Questions to discuss (with answers)
  • Why does the line level off? → Limiting factors stop the population at the carrying capacity.
  • Lower only the water. What happens? → The carrying capacity drops, even if there is lots of food and space. The scarcest resource limits the population.
  • What does “perfect conditions” show? → Biotic potential: with no limiting factors the line keeps climbing. This almost never happens in nature.

🧪 Fishy Population Changes

LabFind the pond's carrying capacityTeamworkSelf-Regulation and Discipline

You need: a “pond” sheet with 16 squares · 40 counters or beans (the fish) · a pencil

Method

  1. Start with 2 fish in the pond. Each fish needs one square of space.
  2. Each round, the population doubles (fish have babies).
  3. If there are no empty squares, the extra fish cannot survive. Take them out.
  4. Record the fish at the end of each round.

Safety: do not put counters or beans in your mouth. Wash your hands afterwards.
