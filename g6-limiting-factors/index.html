Picture words

Look at the picture. Tap 🔊 to hear the word. Say it with me!

💧🌿
Resource

Something living things need.

e.g. Water, food, space, sunlight.

🐟🐟🐟
Population

All of one kind of living thing in one place.

e.g. All the fish in the pond.

🚧
Limiting factor

Something that stops a population growing.

e.g. Not enough food or space.

🚌
Carrying capacity

The biggest number a place can hold.

e.g. The pond holds 16 fish.

🚀
Biotic potential

Growth with NO limits (perfect conditions).

e.g. The line goes up and up.

🚶‍♂️➡️
Migrate

Move to a new place.

e.g. Birds fly away to find food.

5 limiting factors

Look at each card: lots of the resource → many living things. Little → few.

💧Water
LOTS 💧🦌🦌🦌🦌
LITTLE 💧🦌

Dry summer: less water, fewer oryx.

🌿Food
LOTS 🌿🐐🐐🐐🐐
LITTLE 🌿🐐

Less grass: fewer goats can eat.

📏Space
LOTS 📏🐟🐟🐟🐟
LITTLE 📏🐟

Small pond: fewer fish fit.

☀️Sunlight
LOTS ☀️🌱🌱🌱🌱
LITTLE ☀️🌱

Murky water: less seagrass grows.

🌡️Temperature
LOTS 🌡️🪸🪸🪸🪸
LITTLE 🌡️🪸

Very hot sea: fewer corals live.

Remember: the resource that runs out first limits the population. 🚧

Living or nonliving?

Tap a card to choose living or NONLIVING. Tap again to change.

living = grows, eats, has babies 🌱🦁
NONLIVING = not alive 💧☀️🪨
Why? Grass, algae, lions and trees are living. Water, heat, space and sunlight are nonliving. Both can be limiting factors!
