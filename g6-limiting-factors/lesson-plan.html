Success criteria

  • I can name 5 limiting factors.

Adapted for a class of 5 learners with different levels of English: short simple sentences, picture words with read-aloud (🔊), diagrams on every main idea, and one shared worksheet, then Green / Orange / Purple colour tasks (colour only, no level names).

Key vocabulary

  • Resource: Something living things need from the ecosystem: water, food, space, sunlight.
  • Population: All the organisms of one species in the same area at the same time.
  • Limiting factor: Anything that restricts the size of a population.
  • Biotic potential: The growth a population could have in perfect conditions, with no limiting factors.
  • Carrying capacity: The largest number of one species an ecosystem can support over time.
  • Migrate: To move to a new area, often to find more resources.
  • Die out: When no individuals of a population are left in an area.
  • Environmental factor: Any condition around a living thing, like temperature or rainfall.

Differentiation

Green · Support

  1. Complete: “A limiting factor ____ the size of a population.”
  2. Name two limiting factors.
  3. True or false: populations can grow forever.
  4. How many fish could the lab pond support?
Answers: Answers
1. restricts (limits)
2. any two of water, food, space, sunlight, temperature
3. False
4. 16

Orange · At level

  1. Sketch the population growth graph. Label the carrying capacity.
  2. Explain the difference between biotic potential and carrying capacity.
  3. Explain why the oryx on Sir Bani Yas need water points in summer.
Answers: Answers
1. An S-shaped line that rises, then levels off; the flat part is the carrying capacity.
2. Biotic potential is growth in perfect conditions; carrying capacity is the real maximum an ecosystem can support.
3. Water is a limiting factor in the dry summer; extra water points let more oryx survive.

Purple · Challenge

  1. Predict what happens to the pond's carrying capacity if Grandpa doubles its size. Explain.
  2. Why does almost no population reach its biotic potential?
  3. Design an experiment to test whether food limits a population of mealworms.
Answers: Answers
1. It increases (about 32 fish) because there is more space, if food and oxygen also increase.
2. In nature there are always limiting factors, such as water, food, space or temperature.
3. Change: amount of food. Keep: container size, temperature, starting number. Measure: population after 3 weeks.
