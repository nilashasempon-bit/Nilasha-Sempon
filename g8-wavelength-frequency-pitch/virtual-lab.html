🖥️ Stadium wave

SimulationSlow vs fast wavesTeamwork
Questions to discuss (with answers)
  • Slow waves → A new wave starts every 8 s: LOW frequency, waves far apart (long wavelength).
  • Fast waves → A new wave starts every 2 s: HIGH frequency, waves close together (short wavelength).
  • Your turn: class wave → Do slow class waves, then fast ones. Count how many pass Saif in 10 s.

🖥️ Frequency in ripples

SimulationDrops per secondCritical Thinking

The frequency of a wave equals the number of vibrations the source makes each second. More vibrations per second = a shorter wavelength and a higher pitch.

🖥️ Pitch in any medium

SimulationSolid · liquid · gas · vacuumCritical Thinking

A sound wave keeps the same frequency, and so the same pitch, in every medium. Tap Send: a whistle sounds just as high in water or air, but in a vacuum there is no sound at all.

🧪 Spring lab

LabWavelength vs frequencyTeamworkSelf-Regulation and Discipline

You need: a coil spring toy or a skipping rope · a stopwatch · floor tape every 50 cm

Method

  1. Lay the spring on the floor next to the tape marks.
  2. Holder: keep one end still. Shaker: move the other end side to side steadily for 10 s.
  3. Observer: count the waves made in 10 s and measure one wavelength.
  4. Repeat with a slow then a fast shake. Record below.

Safety: never let go of a stretched spring. Keep it low on the floor.
