🖥️ X-linked cross

SimulationColour blindness & haemophiliaCritical ThinkingNumeracy Skills
Questions to discuss (with answers)
  • Carrier mother × normal father? → Daughters: 50% normal, 50% carriers. Sons: 50% normal, 50% affected.
  • How can a daughter be colour blind? → Father XbY and mother a carrier (XBXb) or affected (XbXb).
  • 🇦🇪 Why does this matter in the UAE? → G6PD deficiency, an X-linked condition common in our region, is included in UAE newborn screening, and premarital screening helps families understand inherited risks.

🧪 Hand-span survey

LabMake a bell curveNumeracy SkillsTeamwork

You need: a ruler · the class tally chart on the board

Method

  1. Spread your hand; measure from thumb tip to little-finger tip (cm).
  2. Add a tally mark to the class chart in 1 cm groups (e.g. 17–17.9 cm).
  3. Plot the class results as a bar chart. Describe its shape.
