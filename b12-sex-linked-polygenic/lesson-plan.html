Success criteria

  • Foundation: I can explain why X-linked recessive traits are more common in males and name two examples.
  • Secure: I can solve X-linked Punnett squares and describe polygenic traits and the bell curve.
  • Advanced: I can use twin concordance rates to judge whether a trait is mainly genetic or environmental.

Key vocabulary

  • Sex-linked trait: A trait controlled by a gene on the X or Y chromosome.
  • X-linked trait: A sex-linked trait carried on the X chromosome (most of them).
  • Carrier: A female XBXb: has the recessive allele but does not show the trait.
  • Red-green colour blindness: Recessive X-linked trait: trouble seeing red and green.
  • Haemophilia: Recessive X-linked disorder that delays blood clotting.
  • Polygenic trait: A trait controlled by more than one gene (height, skin colour, eye colour).
  • Identical · fraternal twins: One egg, same DNA · two eggs, genetically different.
  • Concordance rate: The percentage of twin pairs in which both twins show a trait.

Differentiation

Green · Mandatory ★

  1. Why are X-linked recessive traits more common in males?
  2. Name three human polygenic traits.
Answers: Answers
1. Males have one X: a single recessive allele is expressed (no second X to hide it).
2. Height, skin colour, eye colour (fingerprint pattern).

Blue · Less mandatory ★★

  1. Colour-blind father (XbY) × normal non-carrier mother (XBXB): what are the children?
  2. Why is human height not a perfect bell curve of genes alone?
Answers: Answers
1. All daughters are carriers (XBXb); all sons are normal (XBY).
2. The environment (diet, health, exercise) also affects height.

Orange · Optional ★★★

  1. Identical twins 70%, fraternal 10% for rheumatoid arthritis. What do you conclude?
  2. Why did Queen Victoria's sons, not daughters, have haemophilia?
Answers: Answers
1. A big gap (60%): strong genetic influence; not 100%, so the environment also plays a part.
2. She was a carrier (XHXh). Sons get one X from her: 50% chance of XhY. Daughters also got the father's XH, so they were carriers.
