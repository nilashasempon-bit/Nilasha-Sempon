🧪 Pondweed lab

LabCount the O₂ bubblesTeamworkSelf-Regulation and Discipline

You need: pondweed (Elodea) · a beaker of water · a lamp · a ruler · a stopwatch

Method

  1. Put the pondweed in the water, cut end up. Place the lamp 30 cm away.
  2. Timer: let it settle for 2 minutes, then time 1 minute exactly.
  3. Counter: count the bubbles rising from the cut end of the stem.
  4. Repeat with the lamp far then close (10 cm). Record below.

Safety: lamps get hot, so do not touch the bulb. Keep water away from the plug.
