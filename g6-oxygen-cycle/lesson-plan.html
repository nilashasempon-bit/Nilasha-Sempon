Success criteria

  • I can explain that photosynthesis is the main source of oxygen in the atmosphere.
  • I can describe how living things use oxygen in cellular respiration and release carbon dioxide.
  • I can draw the oxygen cycle, showing how oxygen moves between plants, animals and the air.
  • I can explain how the oxygen cycle and the carbon cycle are linked.

Key vocabulary

  • Oxygen (O₂): A gas in the air that living things need for cellular respiration.
  • Photosynthesis: Plants and algae use sunlight, water and CO₂ to make food and release oxygen.
  • Cellular respiration: Cells use oxygen to release energy from food, giving off CO₂.
  • Carbon dioxide (CO₂): A gas of carbon and oxygen, released by respiration and burning.
  • Atmosphere: The layer of gases around Earth.
  • Phytoplankton: Tiny living things in water that make over half of Earth's oxygen.
  • Combustion: Burning: it uses oxygen and releases carbon dioxide.
  • By-product: Something made as well as the main product, like O₂ in photosynthesis.

Differentiation

Green · Support

  1. Complete: “Plants release ____ in photosynthesis.”
  2. Circle: animals breathe out oxygen / carbon dioxide.
  3. True or false: our cells need oxygen for respiration.
  4. Which makes oxygen: a ghaf tree or a camel?
Answers: Answers
1. oxygen
2. carbon dioxide
3. True
4. a ghaf tree

Orange · At level

  1. Draw the oxygen cycle with a plant and an animal. Label the O₂ and CO₂ arrows.
  2. Textbook diagram: what are the missing terms A, B and C?
  3. Why do fish in Hatta Dam need water plants and algae?
Answers: Answers
1. Plant → O₂ arrow → animal; animal → CO₂ arrow → plant, making a circle.
2. A: oxygen · B: photosynthesis · C: dioxide (carbon dioxide).
3. They release oxygen into the water by photosynthesis; fish take in this dissolved oxygen for respiration.

Purple · Challenge

  1. Earth's early atmosphere had no oxygen gas. Explain how oxygen got into the air.
  2. Explain how the oxygen cycle and the carbon cycle are linked.
  3. Design a test: does more light make pondweed give out more oxygen? Change? Keep? Measure?
Answers: Answers
1. Bacteria that could photosynthesise evolved and released O₂ as a by-product; over time plants and algae added more.
2. Photosynthesis takes in CO₂ and gives out O₂; respiration does the opposite. The same atoms move through both cycles.
3. Change: lamp distance. Keep: pondweed, water, temperature, time. Measure: bubbles per minute, 3 times each.
