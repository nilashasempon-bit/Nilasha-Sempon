Success criteria

  • Foundation: I can define multiple alleles and give two examples (ABO blood groups, rabbit coat colour).
  • Secure: I can write the genotypes for each ABO blood group and use the rabbit hierarchy of dominance.
  • Advanced: I can use Punnett squares to predict genotype and phenotype ratios for multiple allele crosses.

Key vocabulary

  • Allele: A different form of the same gene.
  • Multiple alleles: One gene with more than two alleles in a population.
  • Codominance: Both alleles are fully expressed in the heterozygote (e.g. IAIB = AB).
  • Incomplete dominance: The heterozygote shows an intermediate (blended) phenotype.
  • ABO blood group system: Three alleles: IA, IB and i give blood types A, B, AB and O.
  • Full coat colour: Rabbit phenotype from allele C, dominant over all the others.
  • Chinchilla · Himalayan: Grey rabbit (cch) · white rabbit with dark ears, nose and feet (ch).
  • Albino: White rabbit with pink eyes: genotype cc (c is recessive to all).

Differentiation

Green · Mandatory ★

  1. Define multiple alleles and give two examples.
  2. What are the possible children if one parent is AB and the other is O?
Answers: Answers
1. One gene with more than two alleles: ABO blood groups and rabbit coat colour.
2. IAIB × ii → IAi and IBi: 50% A, 50% B.

Blue · Less mandatory ★★

  1. Cross a full-colour male (Cch) with a chinchilla female (cchch). Give both ratios.
  2. Can two Himalayan rabbits have an albino baby?
Answers: Answers
1. Genotypes 1 : 1 : 1 : 1; phenotypes 2 full colour : 1 chinchilla : 1 Himalayan.
2. Yes, if both are chc: 25% albino (cc).

Orange · Optional ★★★

  1. Mohammed crosses a chinchilla rabbit (cchc) with a Himalayan rabbit (chc). What phenotypes can he expect?
  2. Which term best describes rabbit fur colour inheritance?
Answers: Answers
1. cchch, cchc, chc, cc → 2 chinchilla : 1 Himalayan : 1 albino.
2. Multiple alleles (with a hierarchy of dominance).
