Why? ABO has 3 alleles (IA, IB, i) → 6 genotypes and 4 blood types; IA and IB are codominant. Rabbit coat colour has 4 alleles (C, cch, ch, c) → 10 genotypes and 4 phenotypes, in a hierarchy of dominance.

Activity 2: paint pots and a team captain (the unconventional version)

Think of it like this

ABO = paint pots. You get two pots, one from each parent. IA is red paint, IB is blue paint, and i is an empty pot. Red + empty looks red (A). Red + blue gives red AND blue stripes: both show (AB). Two empty pots: no paint (O).
Rabbits = a team captain list. Captain C, vice-captain cch, then ch, then c. Whoever is higher on the list leads the coat colour.

Textbook definition

Some forms of inheritance are determined by more than two alleles, referred to as multiple alleles. The ABO blood group has three forms of alleles, sometimes called AB markers. Multiple alleles can demonstrate a hierarchy of dominance: in rabbits, four alleles code for coat colour, C > cch > ch > c. The presence of multiple alleles increases the possible number of genotypes and phenotypes.

Textbook: coat colour in rabbits · a hierarchy of dominance

From your lesson

The gene for coat colour is called the C gene. It has four alleles: C, cch, ch and c, expressed in a hierarchy of dominance: C > cch > ch > c.

Read, then answer
Q1 Which allele is dominant over all the others?
✓ C (full colour): CC, Ccch, Cch and Cc are all full colour.
Q2 Give the genotypes of a chinchilla rabbit.
✓ cchcch, cchch or cchc.
Q3 Why does an albino rabbit always have the genotype cc?
✓ c is recessive to all the other alleles, so it only shows when there is no other allele.

Let us think

Why do hospitals in the UAE need to know your blood group before a transfusion?

Type A blood has A antigens, type B has B antigens, AB has both and O has none. Receiving the wrong antigens can make the immune system attack the donated blood. So the ABO alleles really matter!
Type O red cells (no A or B antigens) can be given to most people in emergencies. Type AB people can receive red cells of any ABO type.
Blood donation drives across the UAE bring people of every background together to save lives. One community, many blood types: that is our national spirit.

Plenary: mystery solved!

Mystery solved!

The ABO gene has multiple alleles: IA, IB and i. Dad (type A) is IAi and Mum (type B) is IBi. Each child gets one allele from each parent, so the children can be IAIB (AB, codominant), IAi (A), IBi (B) or ii (O), in a 1 : 1 : 1 : 1 ratio. Rabbit coat colour works the same way with four alleles in a hierarchy: C > cch > ch > c.
