🖥️ ABO Punnett builder

SimulationAny parents, any childrenCritical ThinkingNumeracy Skills
Questions to discuss (with answers)
  • Parents AB × O: possible children? → IAIB × ii → IAi and IBi: 50% type A, 50% type B. No AB or O children!
  • Can two AB parents have a type O child? → No. Neither parent has an i allele, so a child can never be ii. Children: 1 A : 2 AB : 1 B.
  • Which parents can have all four blood types? → Only IAi × IBi: children AB, A, B and O in a 1 : 1 : 1 : 1 ratio. Mystery solved!

🧪 Allele-draw lab

LabChance and ratiosNumeracy SkillsTeamwork

You need: 2 paper bags or cups · 4 cards: Dad's bag IA + i, Mum's bag IB + i · a tally sheet

Method

  1. Without looking, draw one card from each bag: this is one child's genotype.
  2. Record the blood type, then put the cards back.
  3. Repeat 20 times. Count each blood type.
  4. Compare with the Punnett square prediction (1 : 1 : 1 : 1).
