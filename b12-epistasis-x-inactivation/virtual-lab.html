🖥️ Labrador breeder

Simulation9 : 3 : 4Critical ThinkingNumeracy Skills
Questions to discuss (with answers)
  • EeBb × EeBb: what ratio? → 9 black : 3 chocolate : 4 yellow. The usual 9 : 3 : 3 : 1 becomes 9 : 3 : 4 because ee hides B.
  • Which genotypes could a chocolate dog have? → EEbb or Eebb (bb with at least one E).
  • Chocolate EEbb × yellow eeBB: puppies? → All EeBb: 100% black! The yellow parent was hiding B alleles.

🧪 Calico coin-flip lab

LabMake your own calicoNumeracy SkillsTeamwork

You need: a 4 × 4 grid on paper (16 "early cells") · a coin · orange and black pencils

Method

  1. Your cat is female XOXB. For each cell, flip the coin.
  2. Heads: XB is switched off → colour the cell orange. Tails: XO is switched off → colour it black.
  3. Count the orange and black cells. Compare your cat with another pair's cat.
