SimulationRipples & a bobbing leafCritical Thinking

SimulationSolid · liquid · gas · vacuumCritical Thinking

🧪 Sound-meter lab

LabDistance vs decibelsTeamworkSelf-Regulation and Discipline

You need: a phone alarm · a sound-meter app on a second phone · a metre ruler

Method

  1. Put the alarm phone on a desk. Hold the meter 0.5 m away.
  2. Timer: play the alarm at the same volume every time.
  3. Reader: record the highest decibel (dB) reading on the meter.
  4. Repeat at a short then a long distance. Record below.

Safety: keep the volume comfortable. Never hold a phone against your ear.
